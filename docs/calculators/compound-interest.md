# Compound interest calculator

Interest can earn interest too. Ask Claude how that adds up over time. You get the math and a chart you can change.

Free in Claude. No sign-in.

## Ask things like

| People search for | Ask Claude |
|---|---|
| compound interest calculator | What does $10,000 grow to at 5% in 10 years? Show a chart. |
| future value calculator | What is the future value of $10,000 at 6% in 15 years? |
| daily compound interest calculator | Compare daily and monthly compounding on $10,000 at 5%. |
| apy calculator | What is the APY of 5% compounded daily? |
| monthly compound interest calculator | Start with $5,000, add $100 a month, at 7%, for 15 years. |
| compound interest chart | Chart $1,000 at 8% over 30 years. |
