# Stock return, S&P 500 and DCA calculators

See what money put into a US stock or fund in the past would have become. Try one lump sum, or the same amount each month (dollar-cost averaging, or DCA).

Free in Claude. No sign-in.

## Ask things like

| People search for | Ask Claude |
|---|---|
| s&p 500 calculator | What would $1,000 in the S&P 500 have become from 2000 to 2025? |
| voo calculator | What would $200 a month in VOO have become over 10 years? |
| stock return calculator | What would $1,000 in Costco have become over 5 years? |
| if i invested | If I had invested $1,000 in Apple 10 years ago, what would I have? |
| dca calculator | DCA $200 a month into VOO for 5 years, with a chart. |
| dollar cost averaging vs lump sum | Compare $12,000 at once with $1,000 a month. |

These use real monthly prices for more than 12,000 US stocks and funds, updated nightly. Past results do not predict future results.
