# Investment calculator

See what the money you add each month could grow to. Change the amount, the years or the rate, and the chart moves with you.

Free in Claude. No sign-in.

## Ask things like

| People search for | Ask Claude |
|---|---|
| investment calculator | What could $500 a month grow to in 30 years? |
| investment growth calculator | Show growth from $10,000 plus $200 a month for 20 years. |
| monthly investment calculator | What could $100 a month become in 10 years? |
| if i invest 500 a month for 20 years | If I invest $500 a month for 20 years, what could I have? |
| investment calculator with inflation | What could $500 a month be worth in today's dollars? |
