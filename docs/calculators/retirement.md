# Retirement calculator

Check if your savings could last. Tell Claude your age, your savings and what you plan to spend each month.

Free in Claude. No sign-in.

## Ask things like

| People search for | Ask Claude |
|---|---|
| retirement calculator | Can I retire at 60 with $800,000 and spend $4,000 a month? |
| how long will my savings last | How long will $500,000 last if I spend $3,000 a month? |
| can i retire at 55 | Can I retire at 55 with $1,000,000? |
| retirement withdrawal calculator | How much can I take out each month from $1,000,000? |
| 4 percent rule calculator | Test the 4% rule on $1,000,000 over 30 years. |
| am i on track for retirement | I am 40 with $100,000 and save $500 a month. Can I retire at 65? |
