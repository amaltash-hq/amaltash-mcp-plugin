# Net worth calculator

Add up what you own and what you owe. Then see how it compares with people your age.

Free in Claude. No sign-in.

## Ask things like

| People search for | Ask Claude |
|---|---|
| net worth calculator | My assets are $300,000 and my debts are $100,000. What is my net worth? |
| average net worth by age | How does a net worth of $200,000 compare at age 40? |
| net worth by age | What is the typical net worth at age 50? |
| what is my net worth | Help me add up my net worth. |

The age comparison uses median net worth by age from the Federal Reserve's 2022 survey.
