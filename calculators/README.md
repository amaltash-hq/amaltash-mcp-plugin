# Amaltash Investment & Retirement Calculators

Free compound interest, investment, retirement, CD, S&P 500 and DCA calculators
in your chat, with charts you can change. No account. No sign-in.

This plugin adds one server, **amaltash-calculators**, at
`https://tools.amaltash.com/mcp`. It is public and read-only.

## Install

```
/plugin marketplace add amaltash-hq/amaltash-mcp-plugin
/plugin install calculators@amaltash
```

Then ask Claude a money question, like *"What could $500 a month grow to in 30
years?"* See [what you can ask](../README.md#what-you-can-ask) and the
[docs](https://amaltash.com/mcp/calculators).

## Good to know

Results are for education only. They are not investment advice or a promise of
future results. Projections are hypothetical. You can lose money.
